Title: Heat treatment and tensile response of additively manufactured Ti-6Al-4V alloy

Experimental summary:
Two titanium alloy samples were evaluated in this study. Sample Ti64-STA was a
laser powder bed fusion Ti-6Al-4V alloy solution treated at 955 C for 1 h,
water quenched, and aged at 550 C for 4 h. Sample Ti64-ANN was built using the
same powder and scan strategy but annealed at 800 C for 2 h followed by furnace
cooling.

Microstructure and phase characterization:
Ti64-STA showed a fine alpha prime/alpha plus beta transformed structure. The
prior-beta grain size was 78 um and the alpha lath thickness was 0.82 um. Its
measured oxygen content was 0.17 wt%. Ti64-ANN showed coarser alpha plus beta
laths with a prior-beta grain size of 84 um and an alpha lath thickness of
1.45 um. Its measured oxygen content was 0.16 wt%. Phase analysis by XRD
confirmed alpha plus beta titanium in both samples.

Table 1. Shared sample-level properties.
Sample      Alloy      Heat treatment                         Prior-beta grain size  Alpha lath thickness  Oxygen content
Ti64-STA    Ti-6Al-4V  955 C/1 h WQ + 550 C/4 h aging          78 um                  0.82 um               0.17 wt%
Ti64-ANN    Ti-6Al-4V  800 C/2 h furnace cooling               84 um                  1.45 um               0.16 wt%

Mechanical testing:
Room-temperature tensile tests were performed according to ASTM E8 using
sub-size specimens. Two strain rates were used for each sample. The tensile
direction was parallel to the build direction.

Table 2. Tensile properties under different strain rates.
Sample      Temperature  Strain rate  Yield strength  Ultimate tensile strength  Elongation  Method
Ti64-STA    25 C         1e-3 s-1      1045 MPa        1128 MPa                   10.6 %      ASTM E8 tensile test
Ti64-STA    25 C         1e-2 s-1      1088 MPa        1162 MPa                   8.9 %       ASTM E8 tensile test
Ti64-ANN    25 C         1e-3 s-1      930 MPa         1005 MPa                   14.8 %      ASTM E8 tensile test
Ti64-ANN    25 C         1e-2 s-1      958 MPa         1032 MPa                   12.7 %      ASTM E8 tensile test

Fatigue testing:
Axial fatigue tests were conducted at room temperature with a stress ratio
R = 0.1 and a frequency of 20 Hz. Ti64-STA survived 1.2e6 cycles at a maximum
stress of 620 MPa. Ti64-ANN survived 1.8e6 cycles at a maximum stress of
560 MPa.
